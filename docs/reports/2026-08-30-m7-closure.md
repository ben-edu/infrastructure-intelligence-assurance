## Closure decision

Milestone 7 is complete within the project's accepted evidence-first, read-only operational-intelligence scope.

The milestone is not closed by converting missing evidence into stronger claims. Where authoritative evidence is unavailable, the platform answers with explicit `UNKNOWN`, incomplete-source state, or required live verification.

Potential controlled-remediation and provider-coupled autonomous-investigation features remain intentionally deferred. They are not required to claim the read-only M7 operator-intelligence outcome complete, and they must not bypass the observation/control-plane boundary.

## Observed risks

- the five-minute operator path now aggregates multiple derived evidence domains, so M8 must harden scheduling, isolation, failure behavior, and artifact lifecycle without weakening trust semantics;
- current local JSON/Markdown evidence storage remains replaceable and requires explicit lifecycle/backup consideration in M8;
- a successful service run does not prove every upstream source was complete; source-level failure/partial semantics must remain visible;
- operational recommendations must remain distinct from approval and execution.

## Intentionally deferred

The following are not silently included in M7 closure:

- autonomous remediation;
- general infrastructure mutation;
- broad control-plane credentials;
- AI-provider dependence for deterministic signal collection or safety decisions;
- high availability without demonstrated need;
- weak probes added only to turn UNKNOWN into a stronger status.

The operator-aware planning adapter is accepted repository functionality and was validated against protected current evidence without deployment. The installed base preflight remains available; installing or packaging the additive operator-aware adapter is not required to preserve the M7 closure result and must not be treated as an implicitly authorized management-host mutation.
